About the project

What is Laurimate?

Laurimate (Laurea + mate) is a social robot assistant designed to help students, staff and visitors at Laurea University of Applied Sciences. Instead of searching websites or queuing at a help desk, people simply walk up to the robot and talk.

🎙️

Voice-first

No keyboard, no app. Pepper listens, thinks and answers out loud while the chest tablet shows what it heard and what it replied.

🧠

Campus knowledge + AI

A curated database of 1,800+ Laurea questions and answers is combined with a conversational AI so it can also handle general questions.

🇫🇮

Bilingual

Speak in English or Finnish. Laurimate detects the language you use and replies in the same language — with the matching voice.

🤖

Expressive gestures

Pepper waves hello, nods, points at its tablet when you ask “where”, and uses explaining gestures while it talks.

📱

Animated tablet UI

A modern, animated interface on Pepper’s built-in tablet shows listening, thinking and answer states with live captions.

⏱️

Natural sessions

After the first greeting you can keep asking follow-ups. After 60 seconds of silence it politely returns to the welcome screen.

1

Wake-up detection

Pepper’s on-board ALSpeechRecognition runs in word-spotting mode with a broad English + Finnish vocabulary (hello, hei, moi, what, missä, wifi, kirjasto…). When it hears one of these words, a conversation session begins.

2

Instant greeting

Simple phrases like “hello”, “hi”, “thank you”, “bye” are answered from an instant cache in under 100 ms — no internet round-trip — together with a wave or nod gesture.

3

Live listening

Pepper records your voice from its front microphone (16 kHz WAV) in short 1-second chunks. Each chunk is transcribed in the background, so the tablet shows live captions while you are still speaking. About 2 seconds of silence means you are done.

4

Transcription & language detection

Audio is sent to Google Speech-to-Text with Finnish as the primary language and English as an alternative. The detected language decides which voice Pepper will answer with.

5

Thinking in the cloud

The question is POSTed to a Firebase Cloud Function. There, Laurimate’s custom AI is given its personality, the current Helsinki date & time and the full Laurea campus knowledge base, and produces a short, spoken-style answer (2–4 sentences, no lists or symbols).

6

Answer with body language

Pepper speaks the answer with ALAnimatedSpeech, choosing a gesture that fits: ShowTablet for “where” questions, Explain for “how/what”, Enthusiastic for “thanks/great”. The tablet displays your question and the answer.

7

Ready for the next question

Pepper immediately listens again — no need to say “hello” twice. If nobody speaks for 60 seconds, the session ends, the tablet returns to the welcome screen and the robot waits for the next visitor.

How to use Laurimate

Talking to Laurimate is as easy as talking to a person. Follow these steps.

01

Stand in front of Pepper

Stand about 0.5 – 1 metre in front of the robot, facing its tablet. Pepper will turn its head to look at you.

02

Say hello

Start with a greeting: “Hello” “Hey Laurimate” “Hei” “Moi”. Pepper waves and replies.

03

Wait for “Listening”

When the tablet shows the pulsing blue bubble, Pepper is ready. Don’t talk while it is still speaking — it can’t listen and talk at the same time.

04

Ask your question

Speak clearly at a normal pace, in English or Finnish. Ask one thing at a time, e.g. “What is the Wi-Fi password?”

05

Pause and listen

Stop talking for ~2 seconds so Pepper knows you’re done. Watch the tablet: you’ll see your words, then “Thinking…”, then the answer.

06

Keep the conversation going

Ask follow-ups straight away — no need to greet again. Say “Thank you” or “Bye” when finished, or just walk away.

✅ Do

  • Speak clearly and a little louder in noisy hallways
  • Ask short, specific questions
  • Wait until Pepper finishes speaking
  • Use full sentences (“Where is the cafeteria?”)
  • Switch to Finnish anytime — it will follow you

❌ Don’t

  • Shout directly into the head or stand too far away
  • Ask several questions in one sentence
  • Push, lean on or try to move the robot
  • Share personal data (passwords, ID numbers)
  • Rely on it for emergencies — call 112

    Operator guide

    How to start, update and maintain Laurimate on the Pepper robot.

    Starting Laurimate

    1. Power on Pepper — press the chest button once and wait (~1–2 min) until the robot says it’s ready and the tablet lights up.
    2. Connect to the same network — your laptop must be on the same Wi-Fi/LAN as the robot. Press the chest button once to hear Pepper say its IP address (default in the code: 192.168.0.118).
    3. SSH into the robot 
      ssh nao@192.168.0.118
      Enter the robot password (ask the project team — it is not published here).
    4. Run the assistant 
      python2.7 /home/nao/Laurimate/pepper_main.py
    5. Check it works — Pepper wakes up its motors, waves, says “Hello! I am Laurimate, your campus assistant. How can I help you?” and the tablet shows the welcome screen. The terminal prints [Laurimate] Listening with Google Speech-to-Text.
    Tip: keep the SSH terminal open — every step (trigger word, transcript, gesture, idle timer) is logged with a [Laurimate] prefix, which is very useful for debugging.

    Uploading changes to the robot

    Run these from the project folder on your laptop every time you edit a file.

    1. Upload the Python brain

    scp pepper_main.py nao@192.168.0.118:/home/nao/Laurimate/pepper_main.py

    2. Upload the tablet UI

    scp tablet/index.html nao@192.168.0.118:/home/nao/.local/share/PackageManager/apps/laurimate-1e47c7/html/index.html

    3. Restart

    ssh nao@192.168.0.118
python2.7 /home/nao/Laurimate/pepper_main.py
    The tablet URL is loaded with a cache-busting timestamp (?t=…), so a restart always shows the newest tablet page.

    Updating the knowledge & AI backend

    1. Edit the knowledge base — campus questions and answers live in functions/data/campus.json, organised in sections (A, B, … Study8), each with numbered { "q": "...", "a": "..." } entries.
    2. Test locally — open test.html in a browser to chat with the backend and check answers before deploying.
    3. Deploy — from the project root: 
      firebase deploy --only functions
    4. The robot picks up the new knowledge immediately — no robot restart needed.
    The AI key is stored as a Firebase secret (GEMINI_API_KEY) and never ships to the robot or the tablet.

    Stopping Laurimate

    1. In the SSH terminal press Ctrl + C. Laurimate unsubscribes from speech recognition, hides the tablet page and shuts down cleanly.
    2. To power off the robot, press and hold the chest button for about 3 seconds until Pepper says goodbye and goes into rest position.
    3. Before moving Pepper, make sure it is in rest mode and the wheels are free. Never lift it by the arms or head.

    Help

    FAQ & troubleshooting

    Pepper doesn’t react when I talk.

    Start with a clear greeting such as “Hello” or “Hei”, standing within 1 metre. If the tablet shows the welcome screen but nothing happens, the program may not be running — ask a staff member to check the terminal.

    It misunderstood my question.

    Background noise is the usual cause. Move a little closer, speak clearly and pause for 2 seconds after the question. Check the tablet — it shows exactly what Pepper heard, so you can rephrase.

    Pepper says it “could not reach the knowledge base”.

    The robot lost its internet connection or the cloud function is unavailable. Check the robot’s Wi-Fi and that the Firebase function is deployed, then try again.

    Which languages does it understand?

    English and Finnish. The language is detected automatically for each question, and Pepper replies in the same language.

    Does Laurimate record or store my voice?

    Audio is recorded only temporarily on the robot to transcribe your question and is overwritten by the next recording. The transcribed text is sent to the cloud only to generate an answer. Please don’t share personal or sensitive information with the robot.

    Why does it go back to the welcome screen?

    After 60 seconds without any speech the session ends automatically, so the next visitor starts fresh. Just say “Hello” again to begin a new conversation.

    Can I trust every answer?

    Campus answers come from a curated database, but the AI can occasionally be wrong. For official matters (grades, admissions, deadlines) always confirm with Study Services or the Laurea website.

    The tablet shows an old version of the page.

    Re-upload tablet/index.html with scp and restart pepper_main.py — the page is loaded with a fresh cache-busting URL on every start.
